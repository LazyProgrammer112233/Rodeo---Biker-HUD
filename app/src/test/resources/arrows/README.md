Arrow pictures for `ArrowReaderTest`, rendered from open icon sets at several sizes and styles
(white on clear, white on an opaque dark tile, and inside a coloured badge), the way Google Maps
puts its turn arrow in its notification.

- `material/`: Material Symbols and Material Icons by Google, Apache License 2.0.
- `mapbox/`: Mapbox directions-icons, CC0 1.0 (public domain).
